What are Profiles + Behaviors (P+B)?

P+B Units determine your CDP platform tier.

P+B Units = Profiles (in Millions) + Behaviors (in Billions)

Profiles — The unified "golden record" for an individual or account after identity resolution (ID stitching). Measured in millions.
• Known profiles: Contain at least one addressable identifier (email, phone, loyalty ID, login ID). Each counts as 1.
• Unknown profiles: No addressable identifier (anonymous cookies, device IDs). 20 unknown = 1 profile for billing.

Behaviors — Events tied to a profile: purchases, site visits, email sends/opens, app events, support calls, etc. Measured in billions.

Example: 7M profiles + 2.5B behaviors = 9.5 P+B Units

What are AI Suite Credits?

AI Credits are a consumption-based unit for Treasure AI services. Each product converts usage into credits at a defined rate. Your total annual credits determine your AI Suite tier and entitlement.

Credits are calculated monthly, then annualized (× 12) to determine your tier.
