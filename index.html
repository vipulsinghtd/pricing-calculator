What are Profiles + Behaviors (P+B)?

P+B Units determine your CDP platform tier.

P+B Units = Profiles (in Millions) + Behaviors (in Billions)

Profiles — The unified "golden record" for an individual or account after identity resolution (ID stitching). Measured in millions.
• Known profiles: Contain at least one addressable identifier (email, phone, loyalty ID, login ID). Each counts as 1.
• Unknown profiles: No addressable identifier (anonymous cookies, device IDs). 20 unknown = 1 profile for billing.

Behaviors — Events tied to a profile: purchases, site visits, email sends/opens, app events, support calls, etc. Measured in billions.

Example: 7M profiles + 2.5B behaviors = 9.5 P+B Units

What are AI Suite Credits?

AI Credits are a consumption-based unit for Treasure AI services. Each product converts usage into credits at a defined rate. Your total annual credits determine your AI Suite tier and entitlement.

Credits are calculated monthly, then annualized (× 12) to determine your tier.

AI Foundry All Suites

Powers conversational AI across Treasure AI Studio, Code, and AI Agents. A Conversation = an average of five back-and-forth exchanges between two entities using standard LLMs. Metered across Studio, Code, and Agent Foundry on Production Instances.
Credits / Month 0.00
1 Credit = 600 Conversations

Agentic Engage Engagement Suite

AI-powered customer engagement via email, SMS, mobile push, and messaging at scale. Email Messages counted from messages sent via Engage Studio in Production Instances. Email Clicks, SMS Messages, and Mobile Push Messages each convert to credits at their own rate and are added together.
Credits / Month 0.00
1 Credit = 1M Email Messages | 20K Email Clicks | 10K SMS Messages | 10M Mobile Push Messages

RT Personalization Personalization Suite

Delivers sub-second API responses with customer attributes from the RT Decision Engine. RT Profiles = collections of customer attributes held in the engine (measured as the 4th highest daily count per month). Personalization Calls = API calls returning up to 20 attributes per request.

RT Triggers Personalization & Engagement

Fire downstream actions (webhooks, messaging, journey entry) when streaming events meet criteria — ~3 min end-to-end latency. Incoming Events = streaming events into RT Decision Engine. Trigger Activations = outgoing events from engine to external systems.
