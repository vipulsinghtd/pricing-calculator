What are Profiles + Behaviors?

P+B Units set your CDP platform tier. Profiles are counted in millions and behaviors in billions, then added together.

Profiles (M)+Behaviors (B)=P+B Units

Known profiles

Unified records with an email, phone, loyalty or login ID. Each counts as 1.

Unknown profiles

Anonymous cookies and device IDs. 20 unknown = 1 profile for billing.

Behaviors

Events tied to a profile: purchases, visits, sends, app events. Example: 7M + 2.5B = 9.5 units.
